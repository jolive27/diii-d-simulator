How the agents work

The Director assigns work to separate agent instances. Physics specifies the science, Software implements approved work, and Validation independently tests it. Files and reports preserve their work between sessions. They are development tools, not characters in the simulator.

When to consider High reasoning

Medium suits infrastructure, routine implementation and test execution. Consider High for subtle analytic derivations, unexplained convergence or conservation failures, experimental equilibrium comparisons, or radial transport design.

This remains an educational model; passing numerical tests does not establish experimental accuracy.
