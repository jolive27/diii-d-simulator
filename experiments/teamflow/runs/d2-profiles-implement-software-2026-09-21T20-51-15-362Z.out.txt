**Open concerns for the Director**
1. **LEDGER mutation criterion conflict.** The spec's AC-4 amended by DD-16 and the change request say each perturbation must give a residual of at least 1e-8. Verification doc s3 says above 1e-10.
   - I gated on 1e-8, and the test fails.
   - Shape mutation (c) gives gas 5.3e-9, bremsstrahlung 2.1e-10 and Ohmic 2.4e-9, so all three fall below 1e-8; the bremsstrahlung one clears only the 1e-10 bar.
   - The other five mutations give 3.5e-8 up to 1.2e-2.
   - The bremsstrahlung and gas terms are physically tiny per step at step 750, so the 1e-8 bar looks unreachable with this normaliser.
   - I need a ruling: the 1e-10 bar, a larger mutation, or a different normaliser.
2. **AC-7(d) monotonicity cannot hold for the reflecting eigenmode.** J0(b1·rho) is negative for rho above 0.63, so a "no value below −1e-14" rule fails on F3 from the initial condition. I added `enforceConstraint:false` to the verification kernel for that fixture.
3. **Interpretations, listed for confirmation:**
   - With `enabled:true`, all ten fields are required. `{enabled:true}` alone throws, and unknown fields throw.
   - A non-object `profiles` value such as `null` throws.
   - `t` in the error message is the start time of the rejected step.
   - The 0-D guard runs before the profile step. The M02 observer only sees steps accepted by both.
   - Sample `edgeFlux` is the prescribed loss of the 0-D state at the sample time. The per-step delivered flux is available through the observer.
   - The shape of the `profiles` block is my own layout, since the spec does not fix it.
   - The verification hook is `withProfileStepObserver`, because the spec says the kernel entry is not reachable through `runShot`, yet PARITY needs per-step profiles from shot runs.
