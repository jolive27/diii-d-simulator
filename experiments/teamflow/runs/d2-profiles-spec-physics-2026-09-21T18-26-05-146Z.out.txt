## Decisions and why
- **Coordinate:** ρ is a volume-normalised radius, so `V' = 2Vρ` for any shape, using the same `Geometry.volume` as `runShot`. The equilibrium ψ is never used, so the profile grid doesn't depend on whether the equilibrium solver is supported. The gradient metric is a closure, `1/L²` with `L² = V/(2π²R0)`.
- **Coupling:** the 0-D N/We/Wi loop runs unchanged and is authoritative. Each 0-D source total is distributed by a normalised shape, and the edge flux equals the 0-D loss terms (`N/τP`, `We/τE`, `Wi/τE`). Summing the 1-D equations then reproduces the 0-D Euler update term for term.
- **Transport:** scalar coefficients, illustrative and uncalibrated: D = 0.1 (0.03–1.0) and χe = χi = 3.0 (1.5–10) m²/s. The heat flux is the n·χ·∇T form, not diffusion of energy density.
- **Numerics:** 64 cells, backward-Euler transport, explicit left-endpoint sources, same dt as the 0-D run.
- **Tolerances:** integral parity ≤ 1e-9 per step and ledger closure ≤ 1e-10 per step.
- **Opt-in:** a separate optional options object. Off means absent, `undefined` or `{enabled:false}`, and gives bit-for-bit 0.1.0 output including `modelVersion: '0.1.0'`. On reports `'0.2.0'` and adds a `profiles` block.
- **Capability:** `physicsCapabilityChange: true`, with 0.1.0 → 0.2.0. Ten `D2-VER-*` check IDs are defined. SHA-256 hashes are marked pending, not invented.

## Corrections to the research note
- The gas shape `(1−ρ)^n` is centre-peaked, so I used `ρ^m` instead.
- I rejected the fixed 100 eV Dirichlet edge as the production default. It would break 0-D parity and act as an energy bath outside the ledger. Dirichlet is kept only as a verification-only option for the analytic fixtures.
- The research 1e-4 parity tolerance is far too loose here (1e-6 mis-normalisations must be caught).
- The energy identity in the research note omitted the `3/2 · KEV` factor.
- The claim that TORAX uses zero-Dirichlet edges was known-of, not read, so the spec doesn't rely on it.

## Open concerns for the Director

2. The profile-on run can stop with an explicit "profile constraint violated" error where the 0-D run completes, for example at small χ or in the all-zero corner.
