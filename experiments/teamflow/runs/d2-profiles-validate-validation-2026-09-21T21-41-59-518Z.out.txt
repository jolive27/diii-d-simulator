**Failures and marginals:**
1. **AC-3.** One run exceeds 1e-9. The error accumulates over the shot and is measured against a very small end-of-shot W_i (0.83 kJ) in an extreme corner. The spec requires AC-3 on every completing AC-9 case, so this is inside scope. It needs a Director decision or a Software fix.
2. **AC-7(b) on F3.** The error is rounding in my test-side reconstruction of the delivered flux; it grows roughly as 1/dt. The production edge flux is exactly 0 by construction, so this is not a physics defect. The 1e-12 bound does not hold over the fixture's allowed range (dt ≤ 1e-6) and needs a Director decision.
3. **Bremsstrahlung shape mutation.** Confirmed at a ratio of 2.12: it is rejected, but only just.
4. **AC-9 differs from what the spec expected.** The spec named the all-zero corner as the expected failure. In fact the all-minimum LIMITS corner completes, and the 31 failures cluster at kappa=1.95. The M02 all-zero variant fails at default chi and completes at chi=5.
