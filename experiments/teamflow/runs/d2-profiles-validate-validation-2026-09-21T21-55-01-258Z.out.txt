**Adversarial cases**
- **ADV-1: shape mutations across the whole shot.** I mutated each shape at 11 steps, not only step 750. Expected: every shape that carries power is rejected. Observed: all rejected; bremsstrahlung's minimum is 2.12 at step 750, so the specified step is the worst one. NBI and ECH at steps where they are off carry zero power and cannot be detected; I listed them but did not judge them. **Pass, marginal.**
- **ADV-2: F3 edge flux at dt = 1e-8, 1e-10, 1e-12.** Expected: ratio ≤ 1 under DD-25 and production flux exactly 0. Observed: ratio about 4.0e-3 at every dt, while the old-bound ratio grows as 1/dt (27.7, 2,753, 278,375). Production flux is exactly 0 and there are no maximum-principle violations. **Pass.**
- **ADV-3: the kappa=1.95 failure cluster.** Expected: every run either completes cleanly or throws the explicit error. Observed:
  - With chi raised to 10, 29 of 31 failing corners complete.
  - In a chi scan on one corner, the stop time moves from 0.164 s at chi 1.5 to completion at chi 10.
  - The stop time converges with N_rho: 2.080, 1.894, 1.812 and 1.772 s at N_rho 32 to 256, heading to about 1.73 s.
  - It is insensitive to dt: 1.894 to 1.901 s.

  So the stop comes from the model itself (the prescribed edge loss draining the edge cell faster than conduction refills it), not from the timestep. At N_rho=64 the stop time is off by about 9% from the converged value. There were no silent violations. **Pass.**
- **ADV-4: conservation at the option-range extremes.** Six runs. Expected: parity ≤ 1e-9, ledger ≤ 1e-10. Observed: worst parity 1.8e-11, ledger 2.7e-12, normalisation 1.8e-15. **Pass.**
- **ADV-5: off path under hostile call sequences.** I ran the off path after a profile-on run that threw, inside a throwing step hook, inside the hook scope, and with frozen and mutated option objects. Expected: identical to the frozen reference. Observed: 0 differences, the hook is removed afterwards, inputs are not modified and the returned options are not linked to the caller's object. **Pass.**
